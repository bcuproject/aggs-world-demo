Ready to cook?

Cook the perfect pot of jollof by catching the right ingredients as they fall.

1 · MoveDrag your finger, move the mouse or use ← → keys
2 · CatchEvery ingredient scores points and fills the pot
3 · Level upA full pot = next level, faster and trickier

Catch in a row to build a combo (up to ×5 points). Miss one and it resets. You have 3 lives, and a heart drops now and then to win one back. Catch a ? for a bonus question: answer right for extra points! From level 2, never catch a bomb: it blows up the pot and ends the game.
